Voxa is a voice-first AI assistant for real estate agents, on iPhone and iPad, powered by AI: you just say it.

Most of an agent's week isn't selling; it's building and sticking to a marketing plan, daily posts, open house prep, replies and follow-ups scattered across texts, email, calendar and social.

Voxa pulls out only what needs you today: Voxa drafts, you decide, and nothing sends or posts without your tap.

Design preview, not shipped yet; prototype screens with example data. Watch Demo 5: you don't need perfect words. Talk the way you normally do, ums and all, and Voxa works out what you mean. It checks your calendar before booking: Friday at 2 clashes with a showing, so it offers noon or anytime Saturday. Pick noon and it fills in an event card: Lunch with client, Friday the 23rd at 12:00 PM, marked Free, no conflicts, with an Add to calendar button for you to tap.

Watch Demo 1: Voxa opens with a couple of ideas, pushes back when it should, and stages events and reminders you add with one tap, with Undo.

Demo 2: a short goals interview becomes a 90-day plan, and drafts sound like you, with photos and sources; you tap to post.

Demo 3: ask about your inbox by voice; Voxa tags each email to its listing and summarizes documents like contract highlights (not legal advice).

Demo 4: Voxa remembers your preferences and you can see, edit or forget any of them; memory storage is off until you turn it on.

Design preview, not shipped: a Home screen with everything that needs you today, plus open house and showing workflows.

Three steps: you speak, Voxa does the work, you approve; what you say is sent to our AI provider so it can reply.
