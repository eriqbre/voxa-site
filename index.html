What Voxa does

Your busiest work, done by voice.

You talk. Grok works out what you need, does the work, and shows the result as a card you can check, edit, and approve.

AI social posts with real sources

Give it a topic, like "Amendment 3 and the local market." Voxa researches it live, cites real sources, and writes a short, scroll-stopping post focused on your home market. It also picks or generates a photo.

  • Live progress while it works: researching, reviewing articles, writing, picking a photo
  • Post now or schedule it to Facebook
  • It never posts on its own. You approve every post.

Your inbox, by voice

Ask for "Colleen's email with the contract" and Voxa finds it. You don't need to remember dates or search terms.

  • Reads attachments for you
  • Flags emails that contain a fully executed contract

Contract highlights

Grok reads the contract, such as a Florida as-is contract, and fills in a highlights card with the price, deposits, key dates, financing, and parties.

  • A draft for you to check, not legal advice

Transactions, start to close

Every step from listing to closing, with each step's documents gathered in one place.

Calendar, tasks and drafts

Add appointments, create tasks, and draft email replies just by asking. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

How it works

Speak. Grok works. You approve.

No menus to dig through. Tap the mic and say what you need.

1

Speak

Tap the mic and say it however you'd say it: "Draft a post about this week's rates," or "What's on my calendar tomorrow?"

2

Grok works

Grok figures out what you mean, then searches, reads, researches, or writes. You can watch the progress as it goes.

3

You approve

The result shows up as a card. Posts, emails, and anything else that leaves your phone wait for your OK.

Privacy by design

Your client data stays in your own Google account.

Voxa is built so that private client information lives in your own Google account and Drive, not on Voxa's servers. Your clients' details stay with you, where they belong.
